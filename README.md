# DOWN Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/down-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more DOWN users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of DOWN's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Say their answer back to them | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Let them tap DOWN once before you ask | +5–12% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Make the 3-month saving impossible to miss | +8–15% ARPU |
| 04 | Turn the selfie check into proof | +3–8% CR to purchase |
| 05 | Catch the people who close the paywall | +3–6% CR to purchase, from people who close |

Every price, rating and feature comes from DOWN's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for DOWN. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
